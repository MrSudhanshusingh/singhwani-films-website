GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Do you cover weddings and destination weddings?

Yes. Wedding coverage, pre-wedding shoots and destination wedding assignments can be discussed. Share your location and dates for availability and a quote.

Do you provide both photography and video?

Yes. Photography and cinematography can be planned together or booked as separate requirements, depending on the event.

What are your charges per day?

Pricing depends on the event, duration, deliverables and team requirements. The price guide above gives indicative ranges for selected packages.

Do you make albums and cinematic films?

Album design and cinematic film deliverables can be discussed while finalising your package. Please mention these in your enquiry.

How can I check availability?

Use the enquiry form or WhatsApp button with your event date, venue and event type. The studio can then confirm availability.
